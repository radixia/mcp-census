Source: `../data/universe/R-registry-2026-08-05.csv`

- Apexes in Universe R: **7447**
- Of those, organisations: **7377** (the remainder are platforms)
- Organisations with a **domain-verified namespace**: **4291**
- Distinct TLDs among them: **220**

Only domain-verified organisations are counted. `endpoint_on_apex` proves a
server answers on the apex, not who controls the namespace, and for a
geographic claim that distinction is the whole game.

**3394 of 4291 verified organisations (79.1%) carry no
geographic signal.** No European claim can be made about them without
enrichment. The count includes six ccTLDs sold as vanity strings, listed after
the blank row above: `.me`, `.sh`, `.to`, `.so`, `.cc`, `.gg`. Every one was
checked against its actual registrants -- `.me` is `ai-know.me`, `foodnear.me`,
`drwho.me`, and at 24 it would otherwise have been the largest European
non-EU entry, ahead of both `.uk` and `.ch`.
enrichment from another source, and enrichment at that scale is a project,
not a lookup.
